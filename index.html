Find the killer

A logic deduction game. Every case is a crime scene with areas, objects, suspects and a victim: place each character using their clues — no two share a row or column — and uncover who the killer was.

  • Endless randomly generated cases, from 5×5 to 24×24
  • Houses, museums, zoos, casinos, castles and more
  • Step-by-step hints that teach the reasoning
  • In 7 languages, playable offline

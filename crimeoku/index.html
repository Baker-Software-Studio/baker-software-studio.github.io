01 · The scene

Every case is a crime scene

Mansions, museums, casinos, castles, beaches, zoos… Each map is generated with areas and objects that make sense for the place, so no two cases are alike.

02 · The suspects

Every suspect leaves clues

Each character has their own clues. Place them on the map: nobody shares a row or a column, and the victim was alone with the killer.

  • CMark possible cells with their initial
  • ✕Rule cells out with an X
  • Press and hold to place them

03 · The reasoning

Deduce, don’t guess

Every case has a single solution you can reach by logic alone. If you get stuck, the step-by-step hints show you the reasoning, one deduction at a time.
